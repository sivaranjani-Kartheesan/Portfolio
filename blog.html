Starting With SEO: What Actually Matters First

By Sivaranjani Kartheesan · Digital Marketing

When a small business asks me where to begin with SEO, I don't start with backlinks or fancy tools. I start with the basics — a clear page title, a meta description that actually describes the page, and content that answers the question someone typed into Google. Most of the quick wins for a new website come from getting these fundamentals right before chasing anything advanced.

  • Write titles for people first, search engines second
  • Fix slow-loading pages before anything else
  • Use one clear topic per page instead of cramming everything in

Why Posting Consistently Beats Posting Perfectly

By Sivaranjani Kartheesan · Social Media

I've seen business pages go quiet for weeks because a post wasn't "ready" yet. In my experience, a simple post published on time does more for a page than a perfect post that never goes out. Audiences respond to reliability — knowing a page shows up regularly builds more trust than any single polished graphic.

  • A basic content calendar beats last-minute posting
  • Reply to comments — it signals an active, real business
  • Reuse and reshape older content instead of starting from zero each time

Setting a Realistic First Ad Budget

By Sivaranjani Kartheesan · Paid Ads

Clients often ask me how much to spend on their first Google or Meta ad campaign. My honest answer: start small enough that a mistake doesn't hurt, but large enough that you actually get useful data back. A tiny test budget over a couple of weeks usually tells you more than guessing at a big number on day one.

  • Treat the first two weeks as a learning phase, not a results phase
  • Track cost-per-lead, not just total spend
  • Pause and adjust based on data, not gut feeling
